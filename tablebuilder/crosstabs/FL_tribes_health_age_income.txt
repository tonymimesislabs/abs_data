Source: ABS TableBuilder - 2021 Census - employment, income and education
Table: AGE10P Age in Ten Year Groups and INCP Total Personal Income (weekly) by SA1 (UR) by LTHP Type of Long-term Health Condition
Counting: Person Records

This file was extracted from ABS TableBuilder using a custom geography that maps
every SA1 in Australia to one of 49 FirstLight community segments (excluding Segment 49).
The custom geography was uploaded as a CSV mapping SA1 codes to segment group names.

Structure:
- Series of matrices ("wafers"), each representing a combination of the wafer variable values
- Within each wafer: rows are segments (01-48, 50), columns are the column variable categories
- A "Total" row at the bottom of each wafer gives the national benchmark
- Blank lines separate wafers

The wafer heading (2 lines above each "SA1" marker) contains the values of the wafer
variables for that block, separated by ",  " (comma + double space).

Dataset-specific notes:
- Person-level datasets use "SA1 (UR)" (usual residence)
- Dwelling-level datasets use "SA1 (EN)" (enumeration)
- Upload CSV field codes are dataset-specific — download an existing geography first to discover them
- Do not open CSV in Excel (strips leading zeros from SA1 codes)
